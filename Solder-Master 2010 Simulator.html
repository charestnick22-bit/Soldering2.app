Interactive Soldering Tutorial

Step 1: Flux & Thermal Transfer

Apply flux to prevent oxidation, then bring your soldering iron tip down onto both the copper pad and component pin until the pad exceeds liquidus temperature (220°C).

Step 2: Molten Solder Wetting & Fillet

Feed solder wire into the hot joint. Watch surface tension pull the liquid metal into a smooth concave fillet around the pin lead!
